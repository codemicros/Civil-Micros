Formula

Wall Volume = Length × Height × Thickness; Bricks = Wall Volume / Brick Volume

Worked Example

A 6 m × 3 m wall with 0.23 m thickness has volume 4.14 m³.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
