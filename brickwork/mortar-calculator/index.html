Formula

Wet Mortar = Wall Volume × Mortar Fraction; Dry Mortar = Wet Mortar × 1.33

Worked Example

If wall volume is 4 m³ and mortar fraction is 0.24, wet mortar = 0.96 m³.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful short answers to common questions about this topic.

Why use a mortar fraction?

Brickwork contains both bricks and mortar; the fraction approximates mortar share.

What about blockwork?

Blockwork generally uses a different joint pattern and mortar requirement.
