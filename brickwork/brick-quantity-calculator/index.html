Formula

Bricks Required = Wall Area × Bricks per Unit Area

Worked Example

For a 20 ft × 10 ft wall, area = 200 sq ft. At 11.5 bricks/sq ft, base bricks ≈ 2300.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
