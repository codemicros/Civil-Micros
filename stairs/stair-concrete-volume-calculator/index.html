Formula

Approximate Volume = Slope Length × Width × Waist Thickness × Step Allowance

Worked Example

A 5.63 m flight, 1.2 m wide and 150 mm waist slab has about 1.01 m³ waist volume before steps.

For structural, electrical, code-compliance or safety-critical work, use this as a preliminary estimate and verify with a qualified professional.
