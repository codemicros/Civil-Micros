Formula

Total Cost = Volume × Excavation Rate + Volume × Disposal Rate + Mobilization

Worked Example

90 m³ at 350/m³ excavation plus 100/m³ disposal gives 40,500 before mobilization.

For structural, electrical, code-compliance or safety-critical work, use this as a preliminary estimate and verify with a qualified professional.
