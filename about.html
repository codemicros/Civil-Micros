About Civil Micros

Civil Micros is a web toolkit for civil engineering and construction calculations. It is designed as a fast static website that can be hosted on GitHub Pages and connected to civil.codemicros.com. The goal is to provide calculators, estimators, formulas, guides and resources that are genuinely useful for contractors, site engineers, students and construction professionals.

The visual style intentionally avoids gradients and over-designed effects. Instead, the site uses white space, construction-inspired orange as a base color, and separate category colors like blue for electrical tools. The icon system is colorful, clean and reusable across the site and future mobile-app promotion.
