Formula

Backfill = Excavation Volume − Permanent Structure Volume

Worked Example

If excavation is 100 m³ and structure occupies 35 m³, compacted backfill space is 65 m³.

For structural, electrical, code-compliance or safety-critical work, use this as a preliminary estimate and verify with a qualified professional.
