Formula

Unit Weight (kg/m) = d² / 162; Total Weight = Unit Weight × Length × Number of Bars

Worked Example

For 16 mm bars, unit weight = 16² / 162 = 1.58 kg/m approximately.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
