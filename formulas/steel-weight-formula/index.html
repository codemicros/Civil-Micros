Steel Weight Formula

This reference page explains one of the most commonly used formulas in steel estimation.

Weight (kg) = (d² / 162) × Length × Number of Bars

This reference page supports the calculators with extra explanation, which also helps quality and search visibility.

Formula

Weight (kg) = (d² / 162) × Length × Number of Bars

Worked Example

For 16 mm bars, unit weight = 16² / 162 = 1.58 kg/m. If 20 bars are each 12 m long, total weight = 1.58 × 12 × 20 ≈ 379.2 kg.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful short answers to common questions about this topic.

What does d mean?

d is the bar diameter in millimeters.

Can I use this for TMT bars?

Yes, it is the common formula for steel reinforcement bars.
