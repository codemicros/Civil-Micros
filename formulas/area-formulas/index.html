Area Formulas for Construction

Use these formulas for room areas, slabs, walls, plots and finishing quantities.

Rectangle A=L×W; Triangle A=½bh; Circle A=πr²; Trapezoid A=(a+b)h/2

Example

A 5 m × 4 m room has 20 m² area.
