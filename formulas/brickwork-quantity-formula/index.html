Brickwork Quantity Formula

Brickwork quantity depends on wall dimensions, brick size, joints and openings.

Wall Volume=L×H×T; Bricks≈Wall Volume/Brick Volume

Example

A 6 × 3 × 0.23 m wall has 4.14 m³ volume.
