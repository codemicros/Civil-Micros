Roof Pitch Formula

Roof pitch affects covering area, sheet quantity, rainwater drainage and appearance.

Angle=arctan(Rise/Run); Slope Length=√(Rise²+Run²)

Example

Rise 1.5 m and run 4 m gives slope length about 4.27 m.
