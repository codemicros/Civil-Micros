Electrical Power Formula

This is a basic coordination reference, not a substitute for electrical design standards.

Power≈Voltage×Current×Power Factor; Current≈Power/(Voltage×Power Factor)

Example

2000 W at 230 V and PF 1 gives about 8.70 A.
