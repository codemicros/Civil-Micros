Plaster Quantity Formula

Plaster calculation starts from surface area, thickness and mix ratio.

Wet Plaster Volume = Area × Thickness; Dry Volume ≈ Wet × Dry Factor

Example

100 m² at 12 mm thickness gives 1.20 m³ wet plaster.
