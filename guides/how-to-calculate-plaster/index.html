How to Calculate Plaster Quantity

This practical guide explains the full process instead of only showing the result.

Area = Length × Height; Volume = Area × Thickness

This reference page supports the calculators with extra explanation, which also helps quality and search visibility.

Formula

Area = Length × Height; Volume = Area × Thickness

Worked Example

Measure the wall area, choose the plaster thickness, calculate wet volume, then multiply by a dry factor and split the mix ratio into cement and sand.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.

What thickness is common?

12 mm is common for walls, though projects vary.

Do I need to deduct openings?

Yes, deduct large openings where relevant.
