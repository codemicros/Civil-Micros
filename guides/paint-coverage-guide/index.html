Paint Coverage Guide

A useful supporting guide for painting estimators and calculators.

Required Litres = (Area / Coverage per Litre) × Number of Coats

This reference page supports the calculators with extra explanation, which also helps quality and search visibility.

Formula

Required Litres = (Area / Coverage per Litre) × Number of Coats

Worked Example

If area is 250 m² and paint covers 10 m² per litre for 2 coats, base quantity is 50 litres.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful short answers to common questions about this topic.

Why does coverage vary?

Surface roughness, paint type and application method affect coverage.

Do I estimate primer separately?

Yes, primer and finish coats should be estimated separately.
