Construction Estimation Checklist

A useful process guide for consistent estimation on site and in office work.

Good estimation = correct measurement + correct unit + suitable allowance

This reference page supports the calculators with extra explanation, which also helps quality and search visibility.

Formula

Good estimation = correct measurement + correct unit + suitable allowance

Worked Example

Measure dimensions carefully, keep units consistent, include wastage, and separate materials, labour and equipment cost.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
