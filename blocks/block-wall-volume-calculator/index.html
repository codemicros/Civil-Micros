Formula

Net Volume = (Wall Area − Openings Area) × Thickness

Worked Example

An 8 × 3 m wall has 24 m² gross area; deduct openings before multiplying by thickness.

For structural, electrical, code-compliance or safety-critical work, use this as a preliminary estimate and verify with a qualified professional.
