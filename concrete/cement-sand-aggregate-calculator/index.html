Formula

Dry Volume = Wet Volume × 1.54; Material Volume = Dry Volume × Part / Total Parts

Worked Example

For 5 m³ concrete with 1:1.5:3 mix, dry volume = 7.70 m³.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
