Formula

Column Volume = Length × Breadth × Height × Number of Columns

Worked Example

For an 0.3 m × 0.3 m × 3 m column, one column requires 0.27 m³ of concrete.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
