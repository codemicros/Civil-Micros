Formula

Footing Volume = Length × Width × Thickness × Number of Footings

Worked Example

A footing 1.8 m × 1.8 m × 0.45 m requires 1.458 m³.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
