Formula

Volume = π × Diameter² / 4 × Height × Number of Columns

Worked Example

A 450 mm diameter, 3 m high column is about 0.477 m³.

For structural, electrical, code-compliance or safety-critical work, use this as a preliminary estimate and verify with a qualified professional.
