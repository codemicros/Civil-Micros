Formula

Beam Volume = Length × Width × Depth × Number of Beams

Worked Example

A beam 5 m × 0.23 m × 0.45 m has volume 0.518 m³. Four beams need about 2.07 m³.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
