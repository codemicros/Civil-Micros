Formula

Approximate Wire Length = Room Perimeter + (Number of Points × Allowance per Point)

Worked Example

A 5 m × 4 m room with 10 points and 3 m allowance per point gives base wire length of 48 m.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
