This calculator is built client-side and runs fully in the browser, which makes it suitable for GitHub Pages and other static hosting platforms.

Formula

Area = Length × Width; Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)

Worked Example

A 5 m × 4 m room has area 20 m² and perimeter 18 m.

For safety-critical structural design, always verify assumptions and consult a qualified engineer where required.
